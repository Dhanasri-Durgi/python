TYPE CONVERSIONS

1. Interger cannot be converted into list,tuple,set,dict
2. Float cannot be converted into list,tuple,set,dict
3. Complex cannot be converted into int,float,list,tuple,set,dict
4. string can be converted into any numeric datatypes only if it contains numerical values otherwise it not.string cannot be converted into dict
5. List cannot be converted into int,float,complex,dict
6. tuple cannot be converted into int,float,complex,dict
7. set cannot be converted into int,float,complex,dict
8. dict cannot be converted into int,float,complex whereas for list and tuple we will get only key values
9. bool cannot be converted into list,tuple,set,dict
